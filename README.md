### 💻 About Me
I am an **Artificial Intelligence Engineering** undergraduate at the **Instituto Tecnológico de La Paz (TecNM)**, passionate about bridging software intelligence, computer vision, and embedded hardware. From developing IoT picosatellites and industrial energy monitors to applying deep learning in marine genomics and astronomical imagery, I thrive in high-pressure hackathons and multidisciplinary research.
